Our Cybersecurity Services

At Mellow Lizard Security, we provide cybersecurity services designed to help individuals and small businesses protect their digital environments. Our services focus on identifying security risks, improving system defenses, and promoting safer technology practices.

We understand that every client has different security needs. Whether you operate a small business website or manage an office network, our approach focuses on practical solutions that match your goals and resources.

Website Security

Websites are common targets for attackers seeking access to sensitive information. Our website security services help clients identify unsafe configurations, outdated software, and other potential vulnerabilities.

We provide recommendations for improving website protection, securing user accounts, and maintaining safer web applications.

Network Protection

A secure network is essential for protecting business operations and personal information. We help clients review their network configurations, identify unnecessary exposure, and strengthen their existing security measures.

Our recommendations may include improving wireless security, reviewing firewall settings, and implementing stronger access controls.

Vulnerability Assessments

Vulnerability assessments help organizations identify weaknesses before they become serious security problems. Our assessment process reviews systems for potential risks and provides clients with a clearer understanding of their exposure.

Following an assessment, clients receive recommendations that prioritize important security improvements and explain practical next steps.

Security Awareness Training

Employees and individuals play an important role in maintaining cybersecurity. Our training services introduce common threats such as phishing, suspicious links, and unsafe password practices.

Through clear explanations and practical examples, we help clients develop habits that support a safer digital environment.
